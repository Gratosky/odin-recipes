Fried Chicken Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, soak the chicken pieces in buttermilk for at least 2 hours or overnight.
  2. In another bowl, mix the flour, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper.
  3. Heat vegetable oil in a deep skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Dredge each piece of chicken in the flour mixture, shaking off excess flour.
  5. Fry the chicken in batches until golden brown and cooked through, about 15-18 minutes per side.
  6. Drain on paper towels before serving.

Enjoy your delicious fried chicken!
