Beef Recipe

Description

Beef Wellington is a classic dish that features a tender beef fillet coated with mushroom duxelles and wrapped in a flaky puff pastry. It's a showstopper for special occasions.

This recipe is perfect for a festive dinner or a special celebration. The combination of flavors and textures makes it a favorite among meat lovers.

Ingredients

  • 1 beef fillet (about 2 pounds)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 8 ounces mushrooms, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 8 slices of prosciutto
  • 1 sheet of puff pastry, thawed
  • 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Season the beef fillet with salt and pepper.
  3. In a skillet, heat olive oil over high heat and sear the beef on all sides until browned. Remove from heat and let it cool.
  4. In the same skillet, add the chopped mushrooms and cook until moisture evaporates. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Spread Dijon mustard over the cooled beef fillet.
  6. Lay out the prosciutto slices on a sheet of plastic wrap, slightly overlapping them. Spread the mushroom mixture over the prosciutto.
  7. Place the beef on top of the mushroom layer and roll it tightly using the plastic wrap. Chill in the refrigerator for 15 minutes.
  8. Roll out the puff pastry on a floured surface. Unwrap the beef from the plastic wrap and place it in the center of the pastry.
  9. Wrap the pastry around the beef, sealing the edges. Brush with beaten egg.
  10. Place on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown.
  11. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.

Enjoy your Beef Wellington!
